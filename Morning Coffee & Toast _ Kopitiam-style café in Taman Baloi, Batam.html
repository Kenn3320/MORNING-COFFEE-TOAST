A Little Comfort in Every Morning.

Morning Coffee & Toast is a coffee shop in Taman Baloi with a modern kopitiam spirit. The menu draws on Singaporean and Malaysian breakfast favourites: toast, soft-boiled eggs, nasi lemak and a cup of frothy coffee.

Sit inside or outside, stay for breakfast or a quick coffee, or take it with you. Dine-in, takeaway and delivery are all available.
